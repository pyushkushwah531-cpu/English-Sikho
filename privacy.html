Privacy Policy (गोपनीयता नीति)

Package ID: com.englishseekho.pro  |  Version: 1.3.2  |  Last Updated: November 2026

English सीखो Pro is an offline-first educational app designed to help Hindi-medium learners practice English speaking, vocabulary, grammar, and interview preparation. Your privacy is completely respected.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage (डेटा संग्रह)

We do not operate any tracking servers or sell user data. Your student name, XP, coins, daily streak, completed lessons, and game levels are saved strictly locally on your own Android device using Android SharedPreferences.

2. Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)

The microphone permission is requested only when you explicitly tap "Speak" or "Check Pronunciation" inside the Speaking Studio. Voice recognition is handled by your device's built-in Android Speech Recognition service and is never uploaded or stored by us.

3. Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Notification permission is used solely to deliver optional local daily study reminders and milestone celebration alerts on your device. You can turn notifications off anytime in app Settings or Android System Settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Ads

English सीखो Pro contains zero advertisements and zero analytics trackers. An optional user-supplied Gemini API key setting exists under Advanced Options for users who choose to enable online feedback; no key is required to use the app.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is safe for students and learners of all ages. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted to external developer servers, children's privacy is protected by design.
